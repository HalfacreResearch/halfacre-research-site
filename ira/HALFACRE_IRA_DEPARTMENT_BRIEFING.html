RAILS
12 SEP 2026

Desk-killer rules already on paper.

A structure that relies on a prohibited transaction is a blown IRA

PROHIBITED TRANSACTIONS

Borrowing, selling to the IRA, personal-use property, personal-wallet keys, paying expenses from the wrong pocket. Account stops being an IRA as of January 1 of that year.

CUSTODY

A bank, trust company, or other IRS-accepted custodian must hold the coins. Equity Trust’s own CryptoConnect page prints that the owner cannot hold the keys.

COLLECTIBLES / NFTS

IRC 408(m) inside an IRA. Notice 2023-27 is the NFT collectible discussion. Not a finished NFT-is-always-a-collectible statute.

BANKRUPTCY

11 USC 522(b)(3)(C), (d)(12), (n). Live 522(n) COLA $1,711,975 from 90 FR 8941. State opt-out still thin.

FORMS

5498 / 1099-R / 8606 / 5329 / 990-T / 1099-DA. Robinhood 1099-R available Jan 31 of the following year.
