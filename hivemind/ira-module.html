IRA module

A spoke off the Hivemind · three wrappers

The Hivemind is the hub. This IRA module is a spoke. Sourced from IRS pages already opened on the crypto-ira desk. Holes stay holes. Mixing Traditional, Roth, and SEP into one “IRA” is a product defect.

TRADITIONAL IRA

Personal IRA. Deduction may apply. Tax on the way out. Owner RMD at 73.

2026 cap shared with Roth: $7,500, or $8,600 if age 50+, or compensation if less.

High income can shrink the deduction if a workplace plan exists. It does not block the contribution.

ROTH IRA

Designated Roth at setup. No deduction. Qualified withdrawals can be tax-free.

Same combined 2026 cap as traditional. Income can shrink or kill the contribution.

No owner RMD while the original owner is alive. Beneficiaries still have RMDs.

SEP-IRA

Employer plan. Employer dollars only on a regular SEP. The SEP-IRA itself is a traditional IRA.

2026 employer cap: lesser of 25% of compensation or $72,000. Compensation counted only up to $360,000.

Do not teach $7,500 as the SEP limit. Owner RMD still applies at 73.
